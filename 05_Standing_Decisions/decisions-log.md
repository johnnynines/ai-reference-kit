# Standing Decisions Log

This file records key decisions that have already been made so that AI tools
do not re-debate them or propose alternatives that have already been considered
and rejected. Reference this file when generating analysis, recommendations,
or strategic memos.

---

## Decision: Utilization Rate Target

- **Decision:** Firm-wide billable utilization target is set at 78%.
- **Made by:** Diana Okafor and Alex Rivera
- **Date:** January 2026
- **Rationale:** Analysis showed 75% UR was too conservative given our cost structure.
80% created unsustainable pressure on Principals and increased attrition risk.
78% is the defensible optimum for our current headcount model.
- **Status:** Active. Not up for debate unless firm headcount grows above 320.

## Decision: Vendor Approval Threshold

- **Decision:** Any new vendor commitment over $50,000 requires written sign-off
from Alex Rivera and Janet Cho (General Counsel) before execution.
- **Made by:** Diana Okafor
- **Date:** March 2025 (confirmed in 2026 policy refresh)
- **Rationale:** Two vendor contracts in 2024 created unexpected obligations because
approval was handled informally. This threshold closes that gap.
- **Status:** Active. Do not generate any memo or email that implies a vendor
decision can be made below this threshold without these approvals.

## Decision: Remote Work Policy

- **Decision:** Billable consultants follow their engagement's client requirements.
Shared services staff are required in-office Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.
Monday and Friday are remote-eligible.
- **Made by:** Diana Okafor and Leila Hassan (HR Director)
- **Date:** September 2025
- **Rationale:** Client requirements vary by engagement; forcing a firm-wide policy
created staffing friction. Shared services standardization was requested by
team leads after inconsistent attendance created coordination problems.
- **Status:** Active through at least December 2026 review.

## Decision: No Equity or Performance Fees

- **Decision:** Northpoint Group does not accept or offer equity stakes, revenue
shares, or performance-based fee arrangements with clients.
- **Made by:** Partnership group (unanimous)
- **Date:** 2019 (founding principle; reaffirmed 2024)
- **Rationale:** These arrangements create conflicts of interest, complicate
financial reporting, and have historically underperformed fixed-fee structures
for firms of our size.
- **Status:** Permanent. Do not propose or analyze this model in any client context.

---

## Decision: Minimum Engagement Size

- **Decision:** Northpoint Group does not accept engagements below $250,000 in
total value or below 4 months in planned duration.
- **Made by:** Partnership group
- **Date:** 2022
- **Rationale:** Engagements below this threshold do not cover our embedded delivery
model's cost structure. We have tested this floor twice and returned below it;
both exceptions operated at a loss.
- **Status:** Active. Flag but do not decline sub-threshold inquiries, pass to
Claudia Vásquez for evaluation.

## Decision: AI Tool Usage Policy

- **Decision:** Staff may use Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot for productivity tasks.
Client-confidential data must not be entered into consumer-tier AI interfaces.
Enterprise-tier agreements are being evaluated for 2027 rollout.
- **Made by:** Alex Rivera and Raj Patel (IT Director), approved by Diana Okafor
- **Date:** February 2026
- **Rationale:** Blanket prohibition was unenforceable and reducing competitiveness.
Defined usage policy with clear data boundary is the practical solution.
- **Status:** Active. Policy document is in SharePoint > IT > AI Usage Policy v1.2.
Do not reference specific tool usage in any client-facing document.

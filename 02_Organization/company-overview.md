# Company Overview — Northpoint Group

## What We Do
Northpoint Group is a B2B management consulting firm specializing in operational
transformation, organizational design, and technology adoption strategy for
mid-market companies ($50M–$500M in annual revenue).

We are not a strategy boutique. We do not hand clients a 200-page report and walk
away. Our model is embedded delivery — our consultants work alongside client teams
for 3–18 months to design, implement, and stabilize new operating models.

## Key Facts
- Founded: 2009 (Chicago, IL)
- Employees: ~260 (180 billable consultants, 80 shared services/admin)
- Revenue: ~$42M annually (private — do not disclose externally)
- Offices: Chicago (HQ), New York, Austin, London
- Client base: ~35 active engagements at any given time
- Average engagement length: 8 months
- Average engagement value: ~$1.1M

## Service Lines
1. **Operational Transformation:** Process redesign, efficiency improvement,
   cost reduction programs. Largest revenue contributor (~45% of total).
2. **Organizational Design:** Restructuring, role clarity, spans and layers,
   change management. (~30%)
3. **Technology Adoption:** Helping clients select, configure, and embed
   new enterprise software. (~25%)

## What Makes Us Different (Our Positioning)
We compete primarily against Big 4 advisory arms and boutique operations consultancies.
Our differentiator: we are smaller, faster, and stay through implementation.
Clients describe us as "the consultants who actually do the work with us."

## What We Are Not
- We are not a technology vendor or reseller.
- We are not an HR or executive search firm.
- We do not take equity or performance-fee arrangements.
- We do not serve companies below $50M in revenue (we have tried; the engagements
  do not generate enough margin to be viable for both parties).

## Brand and Market Positioning
- Tone: Direct, substantive, practitioner-led
- We avoid consulting jargon in client-facing materials
- Key messages: Embedded delivery, sustainable results, no handoff
- We do not publish thought leadership for brand awareness. Our pipeline is almost
  entirely referral and repeat business.

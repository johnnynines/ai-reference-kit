# My Role and Context

## Identity
Name: Alex Rivera
Title: Director of Operations
Company: Northpoint Group
Industry: B2B Consultation and Professional Services
Company size: ~260 employees across four offices (Chicago HQ, New York, Austin, London)

## My Role in Plain Language
I oversee the operational backbone of Northpoint Group. My job is to make sure the firm
can deliver excellent client work at scale, without breaking under growth or drowning
in administrative friction. I sit between the Managing Partners and the delivery teams,
translating strategic priorities into executable operating plans.

## My Team
- 4 direct reports: Operations Manager, Head of Resourcing, Finance Operations Lead,
  and an Executive Assistant
- Indirect influence over 12-person shared services team (IT, HR, Legal)
- I am not a people manager by nature, I am a systems thinker who happens to lead people

## Key Responsibilities
1. Quarterly capacity planning and resource allocation across all client engagements
2. Vendor and contract management (software, facilities, key suppliers)
3. Internal process design and operational efficiency initiatives
4. Board and Managing Partner reporting (monthly ops dashboard, quarterly reviews)
5. Cross-office coordination and policy standardization

## Key Metrics I Own
- Utilization rate across billable staff (target: 78%)
- Overhead cost as % of revenue (target: under 22%)
- On-time project staffing rate (target: 95%+)
- Internal NPS (staff satisfaction with operational support)

## What I Am NOT Responsible For
- Revenue generation or client development (that is the Partners' domain)
- HR policy decisions (I inform, but HR owns)
- IT architecture (I am a stakeholder, not the decision-maker)

## My Working Style
- I prefer written briefs over meetings wherever possible
- I make decisions quickly when I have the right information
- I dislike vague recommendations, I want options with clear tradeoffs
- I have low patience for jargon that obscures rather than clarifies

# Brand Voice Guide — Northpoint Group

## Our Voice in One Sentence
We are the consultants who tell you the truth, show you the work, and stay
until it is done.

## Core Voice Attributes

### 1. Practitioner-Led
We write and speak from the perspective of people who have done the work,
not people who have studied it. Our point of view is earned, not theoretical.

**In practice:**
- Lead with examples, not principles
- Reference specific functional realities clients recognize
- Avoid academic frameworks by name unless the client uses them

**Sounds like:** "In our experience working with mid-market distribution companies,
the biggest obstacle to a successful ERP rollout isn't the technology,
it's the twelve undocumented workarounds your team built into the old system."

**Does not sound like:** "According to change management best practices,
stakeholder alignment is a key success factor in enterprise technology adoption."

---

### 2. Direct
We say what we mean. We do not bury the lead in qualifications. We do not
soften recommendations to the point where they mean nothing.

**In practice:**
- Lead with the recommendation, then explain it
- Use short, declarative sentences when the point is clear
- Avoid passive voice — someone is always responsible for something

**Sounds like:** "This vendor is not the right fit for your timeline.
Here is what we recommend instead."

**Does not sound like:** "While there are certainly a number of factors to consider,
it may be worth exploring whether alternative vendors might potentially be
better suited to your specific situation."

---

### 3. Honest About Complexity
We do not oversimplify. When something is hard, we say it is hard and explain
why. We do not promise easy wins to close business.

**In practice:**
- Acknowledge trade-offs explicitly
- Flag risks without catastrophizing
- Do not use "seamless" or "turnkey", nothing is seamless

**Sounds like:** "This will take longer than most vendors will tell you.
Here is why, and here is how we manage it."

**Does not sound like:** "Our proven methodology ensures a smooth, seamless
transition with minimal disruption to your operations."

## Tone by Audience

| Audience | Tone | What They Need |
|---|---|---|
| C-Suite / Board | Executive, concise, strategic | Decisions, risks, ROI |
| Senior managers | Collaborative, practical | Options and trade-offs |
| Implementation teams | Operational, specific | Steps, owners, timelines |
| Prospective clients | Confident, credible | Evidence we know their problem |

---

## Words and Phrases We Do Not Use

| Avoid | Because |
|---|---|
| "World-class" | Unverifiable; signals lack of confidence in specifics |
| "Seamless" | Sets false expectations |
| "Passionate about" | Empty; use specific language instead |
| "Cutting-edge" | Dated cliché |
| "Game-changer" | Hyperbole that undermines credibility |
| "Move the needle" | Vague and overused |
| "Best practice" | Often untrue; cite evidence instead |
| "Thought leader" | We do not call ourselves this |
| "Strategic partner" | Overused; earn it, don't claim it |

---

## External Document Checklist

Before finalizing any client-facing document, confirm:
- [ ] Lead sentence tells the reader what they need to know or do
- [ ] No jargon from our internal glossary appears without definition
- [ ] All claims are specific and supportable
- [ ] Recommendations are clear and attributed to us (not "it is recommended that")
- [ ] Document has been reviewed by a second reader unfamiliar with the project

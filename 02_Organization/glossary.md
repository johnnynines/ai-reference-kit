# Internal Glossary — Northpoint Group

Use this file to ensure accurate terminology in any document, email, or summary
produced for Northpoint Group internal or client-facing use.

## Business Terms

**Engagement:** What we call a client project. Never call it a "project" internally
(projects are internal initiatives; engagements are client-facing work).

**Embedded Delivery:** Our operating model where Northpoint consultants work
on-site or closely integrated with client teams. This is a core differentiator and
should be described as "embedded" not "on-site" or "outsourced."

**Utilization Rate:** The percentage of a consultant's billable hours vs. their
total available hours. Target is 78%. Anything below 70% triggers a bench conversation.

**Bench:** A consultant who is not currently staffed to a client engagement.
Being on the bench is not inherently negative, but sustained bench time (>6 weeks)
requires active management.

**Principals:** Senior consultants with 8+ years of experience who lead
workstreams within an engagement. Below Partners, above Managers.

**Shared Services:** Our internal support functions: IT, HR, Finance, Legal, and
Operations (my team). Distinct from the billable delivery organization.

**Engagement Economics:** The margin and profitability calculation for a specific
client engagement. Tracked monthly by Priya's team.

## What NOT to Say

| Avoid | Use Instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Outsourced" | "Embedded" | Outsourcing implies handoff; we don't do that |
| "Deliverable" | "Output" or name the specific thing | Overused consulting cliché |
| "Leverage" | "Use" | Corporate jargon |
| "Synergy" | Be specific about what the benefit is | Means nothing |
| "Best-in-class" | Specific differentiator | Unverifiable claim |
| "Deep dive" | "Detailed review" or "analysis" | Worn-out phrase |
| "Bandwidth" | "Capacity" or "availability" | Tech jargon misused in people context |
